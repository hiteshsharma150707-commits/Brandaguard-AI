About the project

The problem we solve

Organisations publish content through employees, agencies and AI tools. This causes inconsistent tone, outdated facts, wrong terminology and unsupported claims, which confuse customers and reduce trust. BrandGuard AI is a central pre-publication check that compares content with approved guidelines and supports human approval.

Core principle: AI assists with detection and recommendations; an authorised person keeps control over approval and publication.
